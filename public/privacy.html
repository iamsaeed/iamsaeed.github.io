Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

This policy covers Ahmad Personal Mail Sender, a private automation tool written and operated by Ahmad Khan. It is not a public product. It has exactly one user, its developer, and it is not offered to, distributed to, or usable by anyone else.

What the application does

The application sends and manages email from the developer's own Gmail account (asklko2004@gmail.com) on the developer's behalf. It is used for personal correspondence, including job applications. It acts only on that single account, using access the account owner has explicitly granted to himself.

What data it accesses

The application requests two Google API permissions:

  • gmail.send — to send messages from the owner's own account.
  • gmail.modify — to read and organise messages in that same account, so replies to messages it sent can be recognised.

It accesses no other Google account, and no account belonging to any other person.

How that data is used and stored

  • Data is used solely to send, read and organise mail in the owner's own mailbox.
  • Access credentials are stored encrypted on the developer's own infrastructure and are never shared, transmitted to third parties, or committed to source control.
  • No data is sold, rented, or disclosed to anyone.
  • No advertising, profiling, analytics, or tracking of any kind is performed.
  • No data from any other person is collected, because the application has no other users.

Limited Use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs by this application adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Retention and removal

Access can be withdrawn at any time by the account owner from the Google account permissions page, which immediately and permanently revokes the application's access. Stored credentials are deleted when access is revoked.
